# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

Record in the headset. No child or other identifiable person, brand, logo or number plate may appear. Say clearly that no controllers are used, that the room view is real passthrough, and that the 3D models are real assets loaded by the app.

## 0:00–0:20 Hook (book)

**Shot:** Real book on a table. Empty passthrough. Two hands open the book. A fingertip rests on a picture. A real 3D object rises from the page. Pick it up.

**VO:** "Nothing on screen. Open a book. Hold the picture. It becomes an object in your room."

## 0:20–0:55 Sofa

**Shot:** Slide a hand left. File explorer. Open the sofa photo. Long-press the sofa in the image. The real sofa model appears. Place it on the floor. Press and drop.

**VO:** "Slide left for a photo. Hold the sofa. It becomes a 3D object in your real space."

## 0:55–1:15 Movie processing

**Shot:** Explorer, an MP4 card. The word Processing, then Ready after frames are reconstructed.

**VO:** "Pick a film. It processes, then it is ready."

## 1:15–2:30 Movie mode

**Shot:** Ready card opens a floating screen. The film plays. Objects from the video appear in the room. Touch one; it reacts. Poke the screen to pause.

**VO:** "Watch the film, and pieces of it step into the room."

## 2:30–2:50 Close

**Shot:** Wide passthrough: sofa and film objects, then a clean room as the explorer closes.

**VO:** "One day, any picture or film could echo into the room this way."
